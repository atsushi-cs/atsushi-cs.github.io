Course Overview

A project-based course on making games in Unity from start to finish. Students pitch games, form small teams, and build them with guidance from the instructors, progressing from a solo project to a one-week game jam to a two-month team project. Weekly labs cover Unity scripting, physics, animation, UI, lighting, and art and environment design, alongside the different roles in the games industry.
